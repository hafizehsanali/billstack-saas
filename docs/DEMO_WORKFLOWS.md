# Demo Workflows

These workflows are useful when reviewing BillStack locally with seeded demo
data.

## Sales and Customer Account

1. Sign in as `owner@test.com` using password `password`.
2. Open POS Billing or Create Invoice.
3. Select a customer and products, then record a full or partial payment.
4. Open the invoice to record another payment or return eligible items.
5. Open the customer's statement to review documents, payments, returns, and
   the resulting balance.

## Purchasing and Supplier Account

1. Create a purchase for a supplier and optionally record an initial payment.
2. Review the purchase detail and its stock effect.
3. Record an additional payment or return eligible items.
4. Open the supplier ledger to review purchases, payments, return credits, and
   the amount still payable.

## Inventory Audit

1. Open Products and select a product's Stock Ledger.
2. Compare purchase, sale, return, and cancellation movements.
3. Open Alerts to review products at or below their configured stock level.

## Reporting

Use the dashboard date filter and the Daily Sales, Monthly Sales, Stock,
Low Stock, and Profit & Loss reports to inspect the seeded business activity.
